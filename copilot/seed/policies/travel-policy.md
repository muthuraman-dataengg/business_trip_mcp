# Business Travel Policy

## Flight class
For company trips where the flight is under 8 hours, employees must book economy class.
Premium economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours or longer.
Business class is permitted only for flights of 8 hours or longer, or with written Director approval.
Prefer itineraries with at most one stop when the fare difference is reasonable.

## Booking window
Flights and hotels should be booked at least 14 days before departure.
Bookings made with less notice require Line manager approval and a short justification.

## Preferred suppliers
Travelers may use their preferred airline or hotel loyalty programme when the fare is within 10% of the lowest compliant option.
Loyalty points remain the property of the employee.

## Travel safety
Travelers must register every international trip with the travel desk before departure.
Carry travel insurance details and the 24x7 emergency assistance number at all times.
Check government travel advisories for the destination before booking.
