# Accommodation Policy

## Hotel category
Employees may normally book hotels up to 4-star category.
5-star hotels are allowed only when they are the conference venue or cheaper than comparable 4-star options.

## Nightly budget
The standard international hotel budget is INR 12,000 per night including taxes.
The standard domestic hotel budget is INR 6,000 per night including taxes.
Stays above budget require Line manager approval before booking.

## Location
Choose accommodation within reasonable commuting distance (ideally under 5 km) of the client office or venue to reduce taxi costs.
