# Leave & Blended Travel Policy

## Planned leave
Planned leave should normally be submitted at least 7 days in advance.

## Blended trips
Employees may add personal days to a business trip if the airfare does not exceed the business-only itinerary.
Hotel nights for personal days are paid by the employee.
