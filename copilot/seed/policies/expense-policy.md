# Travel Expense & Reimbursement Policy

## Reimbursable expenses
Flight, hotel, airport taxi, and approved local transport expenses are reimbursable when valid receipts are submitted.
Visa fees, travel insurance and mandatory vaccinations for business travel are reimbursable.

## Per diem
A daily allowance covers meals and incidentals: INR 2,000 per day domestic and INR 4,500 per day international.
Meals already paid for by the company or client are deducted from the per diem.

## Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of returning from the trip.
Foreign currency expenses are reimbursed at the exchange rate on the date of the transaction.

## Non-reimbursable
Minibar, personal entertainment, upgrades purchased without approval and traffic fines are not reimbursable.
